FAQ

Will it book into Shopmonkey or Tekmetric?

Only if the vendor lists that integration. Vendors that list it are shown above; for others, ask them or use calendar or text-message hand-off instead.

Is a flat-rate or per-minute plan better?

If your calls are short and your volume is steady, a per-minute plan can cost less. If volume spikes with the seasons, a flat or unlimited plan is easier to budget. Try both in the calculator.

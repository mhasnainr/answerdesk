FAQ

Should I use my booking software's built-in AI?

If your platform offers one (for example Fresha AI Concierge), it uses your real availability without any setup. Standalone tools can work across several platforms but may charge setup fees.

Can it take deposits?

Some tools mention deposits (Jvora mentions Stripe deposits). Otherwise deposits depend on your booking system.

FAQ

AI or human receptionist for a law firm?

Human services such as Ruby and Smith.ai's receptionists cost more per minute. AI is cheaper for routine intake. Hybrids like Abby Connect mix both on one plan.

Does it work with Clio?

In our data, Smith.ai and Upfirst list Clio. Check the vendor's page for details.

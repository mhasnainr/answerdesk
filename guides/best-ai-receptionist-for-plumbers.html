FAQ

Can an AI receptionist handle emergency calls?

Several vendors say they triage urgent calls and pass them to a person. Ask each one how it routes something like a burst pipe at 2am before you sign, and test it with your own calls during the trial.

Do I need ServiceTitan or Jobber?

No. Many tools only need Google Calendar or work by text message. Booking straight into field-service software is only possible with the vendors we list as integrating with it.

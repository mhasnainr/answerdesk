FAQ

What happens in a busy month?

Most plans include a set number of minutes, calls or callers and charge extra beyond that. Run your peak month through our calculator, not just an average month.

Per-minute or per-call pricing: which is cheaper?

It depends on how long your calls are. Short calls favour per-minute plans; long calls favour per-call or per-caller plans. The calculator shows both.
